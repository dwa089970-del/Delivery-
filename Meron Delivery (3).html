Made by students, for students

Meron Delivery started with a simple problem at Dire Dawa University: lectures run long, the cafeteria line doesn’t move, and nobody wants to walk across campus in the midday sun.

7 min

Typical delivery time inside campus, from the moment a shop hands over your order.

20 birr

Flat delivery fee, no matter how many items you order from one shop.

Our riders are students too. They know every dorm block, every lecture hall, and which gate is closed after 9 pm.

What we deliver
